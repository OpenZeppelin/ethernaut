A tiny DAO holds a small treasury, gated behind one rule: whoever calls `executeProposal` needs to hold at least 50% of the governance token's supply. You've been given a small starting balance, nowhere near enough to reach quorum by holding it outright, and neither buying nor being gifted the rest is an option.

Complete this level by draining the treasury.

&nbsp;
Things that might help
* Read what else the governance token can do besides `transfer` and `approve`
* The fee isn't free: figure out where it has to come from before you borrow
